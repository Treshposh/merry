Our story

Make Merry Events plans celebrations in Port Harcourt.

Weddings, birthdays, corporate events and private gatherings all start the same way: with a conversation about what you want the day to feel like. Tell us what you're planning and we'll talk you through how we can help.

Why plan with us

Beautiful Events Begin with Thoughtful Planning.

A celebration has a hundred moving parts. Planning turns them into a clear picture, so you can enjoy the build-up instead of carrying it alone.

How planning works

  1. Share your idea

    Send a brief or a quick message. Rough ideas are welcome.

  2. Talk it through

    We discuss your date, guests, style, budget and what you'd like help with.

  3. Shape the plan

    Together we agree the scope, the look and the details that matter most.

  4. Celebrate

    On the day, you get to be a guest at your own event.
